# VayuSenseX Official Data Sources & Citations

| Domain | Provider / Standard | Parameters Ingested | Usage & Citations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Air Quality Telemetry** | Open-Meteo & Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) | $PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}, NO_2, O_3, SO_2, CO$ | Global multi-model ensemble hourly predictions. Spatial resolution ~0.4° (~40 km). |
| **Meteorology & Boundary Layer** | Open-Meteo Weather API | Temperature, Relative Humidity, Wind Speed, Wind Direction, Planetary Boundary Layer Height (PBLH) | Boundary layer thermal inversion analysis and surface stagnation heuristics. |
| **Indian NAQI Standard** | Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), India | Breakpoints for 24-hr averaging periods | National Air Quality Index (NAQI) guidelines, MoEFCC (2015). |
| **US AQI Standard** | United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA) | 2024 revised annual/24-hr $PM_{2.5}$ standard | Technical Assistance Document for the Reporting of Daily Air Quality (EPA-454/B-18-007). |
| **Exposure Factors** | US EPA Exposure Factors Handbook | Breathing rates by age and activity level | EPA/600/R-09/052F, National Center for Environmental Assessment. |
| **Public Health Guidance** | World Health Organization (WHO) | Global Air Quality Guidelines | WHO AQG 2021 update for particulate matter and toxic gas thresholds. |
